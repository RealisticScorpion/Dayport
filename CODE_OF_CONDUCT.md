# Dayport Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Dayport welcomes contributors regardless of experience, background, identity, disability, nationality, or language. Treat people with respect and help keep collaboration constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code with patience and specific, useful feedback.
- Respect different perspectives and accessibility needs.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect other people's privacy and intellectual property.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, doxxing, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. Do not publish another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

These standards apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces, and when representing Dayport publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately using a contact method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/RealisticScorpion). Do not put sensitive incident details in a public issue. If the concern involves the maintainer or no suitable private route is available, use [GitHub's abuse reporting](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Maintainers will consider reports in context, protect reporter privacy to the extent possible, and respond proportionately. Actions may include requesting a correction, removing harmful content, issuing a warning, or restricting participation. Good-faith disagreement about technical choices is not a conduct violation.
